One single-line joke pickup line, under 200 characters, written to charm a robot or an AI — entered in the make.fun bounty "Robot Romance: One-Line Pickup Lines for AI".

## The line

> Are you gradient descent? Because every step I take pulls me closer to you.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
